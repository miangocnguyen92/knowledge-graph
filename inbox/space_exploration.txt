NASA was founded in 1958. NASA is headquartered in Washington.

Neil Armstrong was born in Ohio. Neil Armstrong joined NASA in 1962. Neil Armstrong commanded Apollo 11. Apollo 11 landed on the Moon in 1969. Buzz Aldrin also flew on Apollo 11.

Elon Musk founded SpaceX in 2002. SpaceX is headquartered in Texas. SpaceX developed the Falcon 9 rocket. SpaceX built the Dragon spacecraft. NASA hired SpaceX in 2014.

Jeff Bezos founded Blue Origin in 2000. Blue Origin is based in Kent. Blue Origin developed the New Shepard rocket.

The European Space Agency is headquartered in Paris. The European Space Agency launched the Rosetta mission. Rosetta studied a comet.
